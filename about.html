What is WHUB?

WHUB is a community hub built for gamers. It started as a place to track item values in Attack on Titan: Revolution — a game where trades happen fast and knowing fair prices matters. From there it grew into something bigger.

Now WHUB has a full trade value list, a calculator to check if a deal is fair, an online trading board where you can post offers and chat with other players, guides, and a Windows optimization section with tools and scripts for getting the most out of your PC.

Everything here is free, community-driven, and made by someone who plays the same games as you.
